Questions

Does it really work with no signal?

Yes. Open the app once on Wi-Fi and tap Download offline pack (about 64 MB). After that the recorder, mastering, video tools, Whisper transcription and the Songbook all run with the network off.

How do I put it on my home screen?

On iPhone, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap Install when Chrome offers it, or use the menu.

Where are my songs and recordings?

On your phone, in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use Export JSON in the Songbook to keep a backup of your songs.

I already bought it. How do I unlock a new phone?

Open the app, tap Already bought? Enter key, and paste the key you got after paying.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay $25 once. Updates and the features listed above as coming next are included.
